Frequently Addressed Queries

Clarity regarding our operational integration, regulatory scopes, and pilot terms.

How quickly can an outsourced delivery pod integrate into our ERP?

Because our pods consist exclusively of senior-trained professionals familiar with NetSuite, Oracle, SAP, and Xero, operational integration occurs within 48 to 72 hours. We require read access and trial balance exports to begin immediately.

What is the exact structure of the 7-day free pilot?

We accept one full months worth of unreconciled corporate bank statements or a messy historical subsidiary file. Over 7 business days, our senior pod completes the full reconciliation and delivers a CEO-ready closing schedule completely free, demonstrating our accuracy before any formal contract.

Are all statutory audits acceptable to tier-1 international banking institutions?

Yes. Nexus Financial operates strictly under International Standards on Auditing (ISA), and our signing partners hold active licensing recognized across major GCC financial zones (DIFC, ADGM) and UK regulatory frameworks.

How do you guarantee confidentiality for sensitive M&A and payroll records?

We enforce zero-trust network protocol architectures, mandatory NDA frameworks for all assigned partners, and dedicated isolated virtual workspace environments for every corporate file we manage.
